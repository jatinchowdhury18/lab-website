this is a laplace transform!!

$$
F(s) = \int_0^{\infty} f(t)\, e^{-st}\, dt
$$

and... this is a fourier transform

$$
F(\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)\, e^{-i\omega t}\, dt
$$

here's a wee matrix too

$$
A = \begin{bmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{bmatrix}
$$

and even a little table

$$
\begin{array}{l|cc}
 & \text{time} & \text{frequency} \\ \hline
\text{laplace} & f(t) & F(s) \\ \text{fourier} & f(t) & F(\omega)
\end{array}
$$
